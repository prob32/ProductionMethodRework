Steel per level at progress p is 90 × (1 − 0.27778 × (1 − p)) = 65 + 25p, which is exactly the Blister share plus the Bessemer share drawn above. Iron in follows the same shape, 40 + 20p, and coal stays at 30. Retooling one level costs 800 × 50% ÷ N construction. This is the mod's arithmetic, not a game measurement: the construction rate is a slider, and in game it depends on how much of the queue the retooling gets.
